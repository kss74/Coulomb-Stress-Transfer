description=Coulomb Failure Function (dCFF) stress transfer, rate-and-state seismicity forecasting, and related geophysical analysis for QGIS. Computes dCFF = d(tau) + mu' * d(sigma_n) via the Okada (1985)/DC3D dislocation model, validated against Coulomb 3.4.2 (r=0.983, 99.6%% sign agreement). Also includes Dieterich (1994) rate-and-state seismicity forecasting with catalog calibration and CSEP-style scoring, aftershock Monte Carlo null testing, focal mechanism import/visualization, distributed slip inversion, regional stress inversion (ILSI), and cross-section tools.
version=2.0.0
author=KSarmiento

about=Implements the Okada (1985) surface dislocation model to compute Coulomb stress transfer (dCFF = d(tau) + mu' * d(sigma_n)), with an optional okada-wrapper dependency for exact depth-dependent CFF via Okada (1992) DC3D. Beyond the original Coulomb 3.4.2 feature set, this plugin adds: Dieterich (1994) rate-and-state seismicity forecasting (with real-catalog calibration, near-field exclusion, and CSEP-style N-test/RMSE/log-likelihood scoring), aftershock Monte Carlo null-hypothesis testing, focal mechanism import and beachball/stereonet visualization (including use as source faults), distributed slip inversion (uniform-rake and fixed-rake), regional stress inversion via ILSI, an interactive cross-section tool (mesh/contour toggle, raster draping, custom annotation lines), earthquake catalog import, and empirical magnitude-scaling relations (Wells & Coppersmith 1994, Leonard 2010/2014, Thingbaijam et al. 2017, Strasser et al. 2010). Supports individual receiver faults, equal-area fault subdivision, and QGIS polyline fault import throughout.
